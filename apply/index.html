Things people ask me

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need: a CV, a portfolio, leads, the full job automation, or a mix. We talk on WhatsApp first, I tell you exactly what I'd do and the price, and nothing starts until you agree. Filling this form is free.

Does this guarantee me a job?

No, and anyone who promises that isn't being honest. What it does is put a strong CV, proof of your work and a well-written email in front of the right people, every weekday. The interviews are yours to win, and I'll help you get ready.

Is it only for tech people?

No. It works for roles companies hire remotely: video editing, content, design, marketing, writing, virtual assistance, customer support, data, software and more. Tell me what you do and I'll say honestly whether it fits.

How do you get people's emails?

From public sources: company websites, team pages and professional profiles, plus the company's usual email pattern (like firstname@company.com). Each address is checked before anything is sent, and I'll show you how to do it yourself too.

Will my Gmail get blocked?

It sends a small number of emails a day from your own account, starting slow and building up, with checks to avoid bad addresses. You can pause it any time.

Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. I set everything up with you. Day to day, you check a simple dashboard and reply to the people who write back.
